Cake Zone Kadapa — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026 | Effective Date: June 2026
Cake Zone Kadapa operates the bakery and custom celebration cake pre-order platform at https://cakezonekadapa14.vercel.app. This Privacy Policy outlines our transparent policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you interact with our service or sign in with Google.

1. Information We Collect

When you visit our store or order online, we may collect the following information:

  • Google Account Information: If you sign in via Google Authentication, we collect your standard profile details (Full Name, Email Address, and Avatar image URL) to identify your account and personalize your order history.
  • Delivery & Order Information: Customer contact phone number, delivery address in Kadapa (Co-operative Colony and surrounding areas), and cake customization instructions (flavors, eggless preferences, piped inscription text).
  • Customer Reviews & Ratings: Comments and ratings you choose to submit regarding our cakes and bakery treats.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following legitimate business purposes:

  • To bake, customize, pack, and coordinate local delivery or self-pickup of your bakery orders.
  • To calculate and track your customer loyalty reward points and order history.
  • To transmit order status confirmations, ready-for-pickup notifications, and updates via email or WhatsApp.
  • To protect our application against unauthorized access.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

We use Google Firebase Authentication to provide secure, industry-standard authentication. We do not store or see your Google account password.

We never sell, lease, or share your personal contact information, email, or order history with any third-party advertisers or marketing agencies.

4. Cookies & Browser Storage

We use client-side browser local storage to preserve your selected bakery cart items, custom theme cake builder selections, and current session between visits. We do not use third-party tracking or advertising cookies.

5. Data Retention & User Rights

You have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, or to request the deletion of your customer profile and past order history. To make a request, contact our store management directly.
